Added: `doctor` warns when `docs/STYLE.md` lists target resolutions of another shape than the project's base size while the stretch aspect is `keep` (Godot's default) or `ignore`: the game renders the same layout with bars there, so a screenshot cannot show a different one (`shot` returns the base-size picture and says so).
Changed: the `look` skill takes its screenshots with `-RequireResolution`, so a resolution the engine cannot show fails with `shot-size` instead of returning a picture of another size, and it tells the agent to report that layout as not shown (with the stretch setting) and leave the choice to the user.
