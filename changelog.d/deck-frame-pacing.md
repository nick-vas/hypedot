Added: `deck` reports `deck.framePacing` and warns when the project's frame cap (`application/run/max_fps`) is below a Steam Deck display's default rate and does not divide it (60 on the OLED's 90 Hz alternates between 90 and 45 fps; 45 on the LCD's 60 Hz between 60 and 30). Steam's store page now shows each tested game's average frame rate on a Deck with a histogram, from Decks at their default limit; `docs/steam.md` and the `console-cert` skill say what that changes.
